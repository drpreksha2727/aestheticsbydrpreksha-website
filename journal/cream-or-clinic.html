Cream or clinic? When home care stops working

Search for acne scars, open pores, dark circles or dark underarms in India and the first suggestions are almost all creams, serums, roll-ons and home remedies. Here's what home care can — and can't — do.

Acne scars

Creams and serums can fade the brown or red marks acne leaves. They can't fill true pitted scars — those dents need treatments that rebuild collagen, such as microneedling, peels or fractional laser.

Open pores

Pores can't be "closed", but they can look much smaller. At home: oil control, niacinamide, retinoids and sunscreen (sun damage loosens the skin around pores). In clinic: chemical peels, microneedling and laser resurfacing.

Dark circles

There are three main types — pigment (brown), blood vessels showing through thin skin (blue-purple) and hollowness that casts a shadow. Under-eye creams help mild pigment; the other two rarely respond to creams and need an in-person assessment.

Dark underarms

Stop the cause first: switch from shaving or waxing to laser hair reduction, avoid harsh deodorants and friction. If the darkening is thick and velvety, check sugar and insulin levels. Peels and laser toning can then even out the colour.

RULE OF THUMBIf you've used a product properly for 8–12 weeks with no visible change, it's time for a diagnosis rather than another cream.
