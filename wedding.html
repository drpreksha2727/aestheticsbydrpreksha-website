November – February · Wedding & winter season

Your glow plan, counted backwards

Ahmedabad's winter air is dry and the wedding calendar is packed. The best results come from starting early and keeping the last weeks calm — here's the timeline we suggest for brides, grooms and the family.

Plan my wedding glow →
  1. 6 monthsConsult & start the slow workSkin assessment. Begin pigmentation, acne-scar or laser hair reduction courses — these need multiple sessions spaced weeks apart.
  2. 3 monthsBuildPeels, microneedling or PRP/GFC for hair, timed so any redness has fully settled well before the functions.
  3. 4–6 weeksRefineHydration-led treatments and glow facials. No brand-new actives from here on.
  4. Final 2 weeksKeep it calmOnly gentle, familiar treatments — a HydraFacial-type session about a week before. Don't forget neck, back and hands; HD cameras see everything.
