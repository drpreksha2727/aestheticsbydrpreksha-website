Questions, answered

Do I need a consultation before any treatment?+

Yes. Every plan starts with a skin consultation so Dr. Preksha can check your skin type, history, medicines and goals. Some treatments aren't suitable for everyone, and the consultation is where we find that out.

Is laser treatment safe for Indian skin?+

Lasers are widely used on Indian skin when the device, wavelength and settings are chosen for your skin type. Research on melasma in Indian patients, for example, favours low-energy settings and longer gaps between sessions to reduce the risk of rebound pigmentation. Your doctor will explain risks specific to you before starting.

How many sessions will I need?+

It depends on the concern. Laser hair reduction, pigmentation and acne-scar treatments usually need several sessions spaced weeks apart; glow treatments like a HydraFacial can be one-off. You'll get an estimate after your consultation.

Is there downtime?+

Many treatments have little or no downtime. Others — like some peels, microneedling or CO2 laser — can leave redness or flaking for a few days. We'll tell you what to expect so you can plan around events.

Do you treat men?+

Yes. We see men for acne and acne marks, pigmentation, beard-line concerns, hair fall (PRP / GFC) and laser hair reduction.

What does a treatment cost?+

Pricing depends on the treatment, the area and the number of sessions. We share a clear quote after your consultation — message us on WhatsApp at 77175 67426 to ask about current offers.

Can I get treatments while pregnant or breastfeeding?+

Many aesthetic procedures and active ingredients are postponed during pregnancy and breastfeeding. Please tell us at your consultation so we can suggest only what's appropriate.

How should I care for my skin after a session?+

Usually: gentle cleanser, a plain moisturiser, strict sun protection and no scrubs or strong actives for a few days. You'll get written aftercare specific to your treatment.

Myth, meet fact

"Lasers aren't safe for Indian skin."

FACTMany lasers are used safely on Indian skin every day. What matters is the right wavelength, conservative settings and correct spacing between sessions — decided by a doctor after examining your skin.

"You don't need sunscreen in winter or indoors."

FACTUV reaches you in winter and through windows. For pigmentation-prone skin, daily broad-spectrum sunscreen is the single biggest factor in keeping results.

"One session should fix it."

FACTSkin renews in cycles. Pigmentation, scars and hair fall usually improve over a course of sessions — and an honest plan tells you that up front.
