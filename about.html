Where aesthetics is heading

What's changing in skin and aesthetic care across India in 2026 — and how it shapes the way we work.

01

Skin health over quick fixes

The conversation has moved from "instant results" to safety, patient selection and evidence. Fewer, better-planned sessions are winning over aggressive one-off treatments.

Industry view, BioSpectrum India
02

Combination treatments

Pairing technologies — for example a laser with a medical-grade topical, or tightening with hydration — is becoming standard, because skin concerns rarely have a single cause.

Trend coverage, BioSpectrum Asia
03

Men are booking too

Acne marks, pigmentation, beard-line issues and hair fall are bringing more men into aesthetic clinics — a group that was almost absent a few years ago.

Market insight, Ken Research
04

The wedding-season rush

Clinics report demand climbing in the two months before weddings, led by HydraFacials, skin boosters and PRP — which is why planning early matters.

Reporting, Business Standard
